The flow

think first

Two roles, one shell: a content stream in the middle and a primary action pinned at the bottom, with a secondary button stacked above it. The smart shortcut everywhere is your Google Calendar — check it to auto-suggest or pre-fill times. (AI is parked as "coming soon" for now.)

🗓️ Host — create a poll (3 steps → share)

  • 1
    Setup. Name it, set when the poll closes & the length of each slot.
  • 2
    Times. Pick days & times by hand, or tap + Add → Check calendar to pull your free times.
  • 3
    Review. Flip through days one at a time; confirm the locked times.
  • ✓
    Share. Share the link (primary) + fill out your own availability.

🙋 Participant — vote, then see the answer

  • 1
    Mark availability. Tap the times you're free, one day at a time.
  • 2
    Or check calendar. "Check calendar automatically" pre-fills it — fix anything it got wrong.
  • ✓
    Results. The best time pops out — "Everyone's free Tuesday 2 PM." Host confirms, shares it & adds to calendar.
